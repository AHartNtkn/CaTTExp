# Semistrict definitional equality over CaTT: is it conservative in every context?

## Setting

**CaTT** is Finster–Mimram's type theory for weak ω-categories (arXiv 1706.02866). Contexts are finite computads (globular sets presented as lists of typed variables). Types are ⋆ or `t →_A u`. Terms are variables or `coh_{Δ, A}[σ]`, where Δ is a pasting context (`Δ ⊢ps`), A = `t →_B u` is a type over Δ, and σ : Γ → Δ is a substitution. The rule for `coh` has a side condition on (Δ, A) alone. There is no definitional equality: types are compared syntactically.

**Strict images.** For a context Γ, let F(Γ) be the free strict ω-category on Γ, read as a polygraph. Every well-typed term t has an image ⟦t⟧ ∈ F(Γ). A variable goes to its generator. `coh_{Δ,A}[σ]` goes to σ_*(c), where c ∈ F(Δ) is the top cell of F(Δ) if A is full and of dimension dim Δ (an "operation"), and the identity on ⟦t⟧ if ⟦t⟧ = ⟦u⟧ with full support (a "coherence"). In F(Δ), a pasting diagram, c is unique.

**The Gray variant CaTT_G.** Every pasting context decomposes uniquely as Δ = ΣΘ₁ ∨ … ∨ ΣΘₙ, with objects a₀, …, aₙ. Let 𝔉(Δ) be the free category enriched in (strict ω-categories, lax Gray tensor) on Δ. Its homs are
`𝔉(Δ)(aᵢ, aⱼ) = ν(λΘᵢ₊₁ ⊗ … ⊗ λΘⱼ)`,
with ν and λ as in Steiner (*Omega-categories and chain complexes*, HHA 2004) and ⊗ the Steiner–Ara–Maltsiniotis tensor. For a general finite computad Γ, 𝔉(Γ) is the free such enriched category on Γ. CaTT_G has the syntax and rules of CaTT, except that the `coh` side condition is read off 𝔉(Δ). (Δ, t → u) is accepted iff either ⟦t⟧ = ∂⁻τ_Δ and ⟦u⟧ = ∂⁺τ_Δ, where τ_Δ is the total cell (the tensor of the composites of the Θᵢ), or ⟦t⟧ = ⟦u⟧ and its support in the Steiner bases is everything. In 𝔉, composition is strictly associative and unital, but the interchanger of two cells in adjacent homs is a non-invertible generator. For example, over Δ = ΣD¹ ∨ ΣD¹ the coherence of type (f ◁ β) ; (α ▷ g′) → (α ▷ g) ; (f′ ◁ β) is accepted and its reverse is not. Strict images ⟦t⟧ ∈ 𝔉(Γ) are defined as for CaTT.

**Strictly unital and associative equality ≡_sua** (Finster–Rice–Vicary, arXiv 2302.05303, §2.5 and §3). This is the congruence generated by three rules:
- *disc removal*: `coh_{Dⁿ, Uⁿ}[⟨A, s⟩] ≡ s`;
- *endo-coherence removal*: `coh_{Δ, s →_A s}[σ] ≡ 1_{A[σ], s[σ]}`;
- *insertion*: `coh_{S, A}[σ] ≡ coh_{S ≪_P T, A[κ]}[σ ≪_P τ]`, whenever the locally maximal argument σ(P) is the standard composite C_T[τ] (FRV Defs. 18–29; insertion subsumes pruning of identities).

Catt_sua is CaTT plus ≡_sua and the conversion rule (Γ ⊢ t : A, A ≡ B ⇒ Γ ⊢ t : B). For CaTT_G, restrict the rules to redexes whose standard composites exist in CaTT_G. These are the composites over pasting diagrams whose trees have at most one non-leaf child at every node, so that no interchanger is involved. Write ≡_𝔉 for equality of images in 𝔉(Γ). Then ≡_sua ⊆ ≡_𝔉 on CaTT_G-typed terms, and ≡_𝔉 makes associators and unitors trivial but never an interchanger.

## Known

1. **Reduction to relative coherence.** Let T be CaTT or CaTT_G, and ≈ an equivalence on terms and types that is a congruence for the term formers, is stable under substitution, and does not change the side condition. Let T_≈ be T plus the conversion rule along ≈. In a fixed context Γ the following are equivalent:
   - (a) T_≈ is conservative over T for types of dimension ≤ n + 1: if a T-type B over Γ is inhabited in T_≈, it is inhabited in T, by a term ≈ to the given one;
   - (b) **EL(≤ n) at Γ**: for all parallel T-terms u, u′ of dimension ≤ n over Γ with u ≈ u′, there are T-terms e : u → u′ and e′ : u′ → u with e ≈ 1_u and e′ ≈ 1_{u′}.

   (b) ⇒ (a) is an induction on T_≈-derivations. Its only nontrivial step moves a term to a ≈-equal type by whiskering with the cells given by (b), level by level from the lowest differing boundary. (a) ⇒ (b) converts the identity 1_u : u → u to the type u → u′.
2. **Relative coherence for full strictness in CaTT** (≈ = equality in F(Γ); unpublished, proofs available):
   - EL(1) and EL(2) hold in every context.
   - EL(n) holds for every n in every finite strong-Steiner (strongly loop-free) context, pasting contexts included.
   - EL(3) **fails** in the context (x, a : 1ₓ → 1ₓ). The obstruction is Eckmann–Hilton.

   So CaTT plus full strict equality is conservative in strong-Steiner contexts and through dimension 3 everywhere, and not conservative in general.
3. **Literature on ≡_sua.** Catt_su is conservative over Catt for terms over pasting diagrams (Finster–Reutter–Rice–Vicary, arXiv 2007.08307). For Catt_sua, "partial rehydration" over pasting contexts is in Rice's thesis (arXiv 2502.17068, Ch. 4), which also discusses obstructions to rehydration in a generic context. Neither proves or conjectures conservativity in arbitrary contexts. Since ≡_sua ⊆ equality in F(Γ), item 2 gives conservativity of Catt_sua over Catt in every strong-Steiner context and through dimension 3 everywhere.
4. **For CaTT_G with ≡_𝔉,** EL holds in every pasting context: a coherence-case coherence over the variables of u connects u and u′. Arbitrary contexts are open.
5. **Semantic background.** Gordon–Power–Street: every tricategory is triequivalent to a Gray-category (strict associativity and units, weak interchange). Simpson (*Homotopy types of strict 3-groupoids*, 1998) conjectured that weakening any one of the three structures suffices. Joyal–Kock: strict interchange with weak units suffices in dimension 3; strict units with strict interchange does not.

## Questions

**Q1 (main).** Prove, for every finite context Γ and every dimension n, that EL(n) holds at Γ in each of the following cases, or give a counterexample:
- (i) T = CaTT and ≈ = ≡_sua. Equivalently, Catt_sua is conservative over Catt in every context and every dimension.
- (ii) T = CaTT_G and ≈ = ≡_𝔉. Equivalently, CaTT_G with associators and unitors made definitional, but not interchange, is conservative in every context.

A uniform proof covering any CaTT-style theory whose side condition is read off a family of strictly associative and unital free structures (with whatever weak or directed interchange those structures have) is preferred to two separate proofs.

**Q2.** If either part of Q1 fails, determine exactly which contexts it holds in. Is it loop-free contexts, homotopical polygraphs, or contexts with no generator from an identity to an identity? Give the minimal counterexample. In particular, does the Eckmann–Hilton obstruction of Known 2 survive when only associators and unitors are strict? In (x, a : 1ₓ → 1ₓ), are there parallel 3-terms that are ≡_sua-equal (respectively ≡_𝔉-equal in CaTT_G) but not connected by any 4-term?

**Q3 (fallback, only if Q1 and Q2 are out of reach).** Settle Q1 (i) and (ii) for terms of dimension ≤ 4 in every context.
