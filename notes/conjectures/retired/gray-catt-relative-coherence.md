# Relative coherence for a Gray-style variant of CaTT on pasting contexts

## Setting

**Contexts and terms.** We use the syntax of the type theory CaTT (Finster–Mimram, "A type-theoretical definition of weak ω-categories", LICS 2017): types ⋆ and t →_A u, terms are variables or coherences coh_{Δ, t →_A u}[σ], where Δ is a pasting context (a globular pasting diagram, i.e. a Batanin tree) and σ a substitution into the current context. The typing rules are CaTT's, except for the side condition on coh, defined below. Substitution is admissible for any side condition checked on (Δ, t →_A u) alone.

**The free Gray-enriched category on a pasting context.** Every pasting context decomposes uniquely as Δ = ΣΘ₁ ∨ … ∨ ΣΘₙ (wedge of suspensions), with objects a₀, …, aₙ. Let λΘ be the Steiner complex of the globular pasting diagram Θ (basis: the variables of Θ, dimension shifted down by one). Let

  K_{ij}(Δ) = λΘ_{i+1} ⊗ … ⊗ λΘ_j    (Steiner tensor product, factors in this order),

a strong Steiner complex (Ara–Maltsiniotis), and let 𝔉(Δ) be the free category enriched in (strict ω-categories, Gray tensor ⊗) on the chain of hom-generators ν(λΘ₁), …, ν(λΘₙ); its homs are 𝔉(Δ)(aᵢ, aⱼ) = ν(K_{ij}(Δ)). Basis elements of K_{ij} are tuples θ_{i+1} ⊗ … ⊗ θ_j of variables. The **total cell** τ_Δ = ⟨Θ₁⟩ ⊗ … ⊗ ⟨Θₙ⟩ is the tensor of the composites of the Θₖ.

Every term t over Δ has a strict image ⟦t⟧, a cell of 𝔉(Δ): a variable goes to its generator, and coh_{Δ′, A}[σ] goes to the image of (τ_{Δ′} in the operation case, an identity in the coherence case) under the strict Gray functor 𝔉(Δ′) → 𝔉(Δ) induced by σ. The **support** supp(t : A) is the downward-closed set of basis elements used by ⟦t⟧ and its iterated boundaries.

**Side condition.** coh_{Δ, t →_A u} is accepted iff
- (operation) dim(t →_A u) = dim τ_Δ, supp(t : A) = supp(∂⁻τ_Δ) and supp(u : A) = supp(∂⁺τ_Δ); or
- (coherence) supp(t : A) = supp(u : A) = the whole basis of K_{0n}(Δ).

On a single hom (n = 1) this is exactly CaTT. Across objects interchange is a directed cell: over ΣD¹ ∨ ΣD¹ the coherence (f◁β);(α▷g′) → (α▷g);(f′◁β) is accepted and its reverse is not. Every derivable term has a strict image, and interpretations respect boundaries (soundness in strict Gray-enriched categories).

**Known.** For plain CaTT (side condition computed in the free strict ω-category ν(λΔ)), parallel terms with equal strict images in a pasting context are connected by terms whose images are identities (relative coherence holds in every strong-Steiner context). In the theory above, the coherence case can fail where the plain-CaTT argument would use it: over Δ = ΣD¹ ∨ ΣD¹ the terms βα = (f◁β);(α▷g′) and βα₂ = (1_{f;g};(f◁β));(α▷g′) are parallel with equal images, together use every variable, and their images miss the top basis element α⊗β; so no single coherence has type βα → βα₂, although a composite of two coherences (a unitor whiskered by α▷g′) does.

## Questions

1. **(Main.)** Prove or refute: for every pasting context Δ and every pair of parallel derivable terms u, u′ : A over Δ with ⟦u⟧ = ⟦u′⟧ in 𝔉(Δ), there is a derivable term e : u →_A u′ (in every dimension). If true, show moreover that e can be chosen with ⟦e⟧ an identity. If false, give the smallest counterexample you can, with a proof that no derivable term of type u → u′ exists (for example a model of the theory in which the two interpretations are not connected).

2. **(Equivalent form, if useful.)** Prove or refute: for every pasting context Δ, every derivable type t →_A u over Δ and every cell c : ⟦t⟧ → ⟦u⟧ of 𝔉(Δ), there is a derivable term w : t →_A u with ⟦w⟧ = c. (Question 1 is the case c = identity; conversely, every cell of 𝔉(Δ) is the image of some derivable term, since ν(K) is generated by atoms and each atom is the image of an operation-case coherence over a wedge of suspended discs, so 2 follows from 1 by retyping along identity-image cells.)

3. **(Only if 1 is false.)** Characterise the pairs (u, u′) for which a connecting term exists, or show that adding the coherence case "⟦t⟧ = ⟦u⟧ and every variable of Δ occurs in supp(t : A) and supp(u : A)" is the least modification of the side condition that makes 1 true.
