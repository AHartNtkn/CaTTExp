# Inhabitation by forced lifts in free higher categories with directed interchange

*A self-contained problem. The theories below are defined in full in the repository files `notes/conjectures/answered/lax-interchange-every-dimension-r5-2026-11-20.md` (Responses 1–34, in particular Response 10 for the raising recursion), `-r8-` (Response 1) and `-r10-` (Responses 1–3, 15); §1 fixes the notation and lists what is proved.*

**What we are asking for.** In the free algebras F_{V+S}(Γ) below, the cells obtained from *forced* lifts (§2) by composition and substitution do not exhaust all cells: from degree six on, an auxiliary bridge of the raising recursion is missed (known result (N1) below). We ask whether they nevertheless reach **some** cell between every pair of generated cells that F_{V+S}(Γ) connects, i.e. whether every hom-type between generated cells that is inhabited in F_{V+S}(Γ) is inhabited by a generated cell. The main question asks for the general statement, for every pasting diagram, every dimension and every setting, or a counterexample.

## 1. Setting

**Composition.** Reflexive globular sets with partial compositions ∗ₖ of n-cells along k-cells (k < n), written in diagrammatic order, strictly associative and unital, with whiskering by lower-dimensional cells strictly functorial (ω-sesquicategories). For n-cells in interchange position along k < l, `L = (a ∗ₗ b) ∗ₖ (c ∗ₗ d)` and `R = (a ∗ₖ c) ∗ₗ (b ∗ₖ d)`. A strict ω-category requires L = R.

**The signature.** The T₁₂ signature of the cited round-5 answers: the sesquicategory operations; for every k < l an elementary interchanger, which for (l+1)-cells F : f ⇒ f′ and G : g ⇒ g′ in interchange position along a k-cell is the (l+2)-cell
```
ι(F, G) : (f ◁ G) ∗ₗ (F ▷ g′)  ⇛  (F ▷ g) ∗ₗ (f′ ◁ G);
```
one naturality cell per varying argument, with Leibniz-signed orientations; higher cells between routes of lower ones; and the auxiliary operations of the completion (binary joins h(A, B), lifts W(A, B) of binary globe tensors, H_L, H_R, K, J, shifted joins).

**Requirements.** A strict theory on this signature is *admissible* if its free objects F(Γ) on finite globular pasting diagrams Γ satisfy:
1. an explicit combinatorial description by generators with explicit boundaries;
2. two cells of the same dimension using the same generators, in an expanded basis, are equal;
3. every elementary interchanger exists and its reverse does not;
4. making every interchanger along a k-cell with k ≥ 1 an identity gives the free Gray-enriched category 𝔉(Γ) (homs ν(λΘᵢ₊₁ ⊗ … ⊗ λΘⱼ) for Γ = ΣΘ₁ ∨ … ∨ ΣΘₙ, Steiner's λ, ⊗, ν), and making every interchanger an identity gives the free strict ω-category ν(λΓ);
5. closure under substitution of cells for generators.

Write π₁ : F(Γ) → 𝔉(Γ) and π₀ : F(Γ) → ν(λΓ) for the two quotient maps of requirement 4, and π_G : 𝔉(Γ) → ν(λΓ) for the Gray-to-strict collapse.

**Letters.** Every d-cell of F(Γ) is a ∗_{d−1}-path of *contextual letters* (a d-dimensional operation applied to arguments, whiskered by lower cells). π₁ sends each letter to a whiskered generator of 𝔉(Γ) (an **outer** letter) or to an identity (an **inner** letter). Inner letters include the elementary interchangers along k ≥ 1 and their naturality and route cells. Outer letters include the whiskered variables, the cylinders of a moving globe against an edge (naturality cells r, t of the cut-0 crossing), the joins h(A, B) and the lifts W(A, B).


**Known results (all proved).**
- **(M1) The least theory V.** There is an admissible theory V (the forced base B plus the guarded round-5 equations) such that for every admissible E and every Γ the identity on letters gives a generator-preserving map F_V(Γ) → F_E(Γ) sending cells to cells and equalities to equalities. B contains the structural theory, the support-free presentation P of the cited answers, the face and unit declarations (an interchanger with an identity input is an identity), and the outer unary subdivisions in both variables at cut 0 (a cylinder of a composite moving globe, or against a composite edge, is the paste of the cylinders). (r8 Response 1.)
- **(M2) The two visible switches.** The left split of the inner interchanger ("inner composite-crossing law": ι(F₁ ∗ F₂, G) is the paste of ι(F₁, G) and ι(F₂, G)) and the right split R (the same for a composite right input) are each visible on pasting diagrams and independent. For S ⊆ {left split, R}, V + S is admissible. In V the binary splits at inner cuts are absent and the outer binary subdivisions are present only in guarded form. (r8 Response 1; r9 Responses 2–5.)
- **(M3) The strip.** On the strip C_{2,1} (inner cells A₁, A₂ composable along a cell, B in interchange position with both), the direct crossing u = ι(A₁ ∗ A₂, B) and the paste v of ι(A₁, B) and ι(A₂, B) are parallel, π₁(u) = π₁(v), and in F_{V+S}(C_{2,1}) with the left split not in S there is no cell u ⇛ v and no cell v ⇛ u. This persists after formally inverting every letter sent to an identity by π₁.
- **(M4) Loops.** On Σ[2] ∨ Σ[2] (two composable 2-cells a₁, a₂ in one hom and b₁, b₂ in the next), V has a 4-cell H : w ⇛ w, a whiskered elementary inner interchanger, where w is the composite interchanger of a₁a₂ past b₁b₂. Its powers are pairwise distinct, and H and 1_w are not connected by any cell, even after formally inverting every letter sent to an identity by π₁.
- **(M5) Positivity.** Every non-identity cell of F_{V+S}(Γ) has positive conserved weight, so F_{V+S}(Γ) has no non-identity invertible cell. (r5 (K50).)
- **(M6) Decidability.** For every S, Γ and degree, the vertices (cells of F_{V+S}(Γ) of a given dimension with fixed lower faces and weight) form a finite computable set, the generating letters between them are finite and listable, and equality of cells is decidable. (r10 Response 3.)
- **(N1) Forced generation fails from degree six.** Every native letter through degree five is forced on its own arity, and every cell through degree five is generated (G below). On Γ = ΣD⁴ ∨ ΣD⁴ with hom-degree-4 inputs X, Y, the auxiliary bridge B = b₄¹ of the raising recursion (r5 Response 10) is a hom-5 letter with boundary (1) D⁰⁰Y_v⁰X_v¹ → X_u⁰D¹⁰Y_v¹ (D the positive hom-4 face of the (3,3) seed, X_u, X_v, Y_v pure insertions, superscripts layers). A second simple witness of the same boundary is (2): the forward elementary exchange D⁰⁰Y_v⁰X_v¹ → D⁰⁰X_v⁰Y_v¹ followed by b Y_v¹, where b : D⁰⁰X_v⁰ → X_u⁰D¹⁰ is the bridge of the same row on the smaller arity ΣD⁴ ∨ ΣD³. Both have the same Gray image and full support; by rigid factorization (r10 Response 2) the number of proper occurrences of the constructor B is an invariant, 1 for (1) and 0 for (2). Every generated cell of degree six has B-count 0, so B ∉ G although its faces are in G. Suspension gives failures in every degree ≥ 6.
- **(N2) Unrestricted generation holds.** If clause 3 below is weakened to allow the value of any witness, every cell is generated (G⁺ = F), because each native letter's single step is a witness on its own arity.
- **(N3) Generic forcedness is decidable per letter.** For a native tag L, form the finite (M6) multigraph at its generic source and target, delete the direct edges whose value equals L, and test reachability of the target: L is forced iff the target is unreachable (and the endpoints differ).

## 2. Definitions

Fix S and a finite pasting diagram Γ, and write F = F_{V+S}(Γ) and π = π₁ : F → 𝔉(Γ).

- **Support.** For a cell e of 𝔉(Γ), supp(e) is the downward-closed set of Steiner basis elements of 𝔉(Γ) used by e and its iterated faces. A cell e has *full support* if supp(e) contains every generator of Γ.
- **Witness.** For (d−1)-cells x, y of F, a *witness* x → y is a ∗_{d−1}-path of d-dimensional contextual letters ℓ₁ ⋯ ℓ_m from x to y (m ≥ 0) such that the union of supp(π x), supp(π ℓᵢ) and supp(π y) is full. Its *value* is the composite cell of F. It is *simple* if its vertices x, ∂⁺ℓ₁, …, ∂⁺ℓ_m are pairwise distinct. (Every witness becomes a simple witness by deleting cycles: a cycle projects to an identity of the loop-free 𝔉(Γ), so the support is unchanged.)
- **Forced.** The pair (x, y) is *forced*, with value c, if a witness x → y exists and every simple witness x → y has value c. If x = y, the empty path is the only simple witness, so (x, x) is forced with value 1_x whenever supp(π x) is full.
- **Generated cells.** Let G(Γ) be the smallest family of cells of the free algebras F_{V+S}(Δ), over all finite pasting diagrams Δ, such that:
  1. every generator of Δ is in G(Δ);
  2. G is closed under the compositions ∗ₖ and under whiskering;
  3. if (x, y) is forced in F_{V+S}(Δ) with value c, and x, y ∈ G(Δ), then c ∈ G(Δ);
  4. if c ∈ G(Δ) and σ : F_{V+S}(Δ) → F_{V+S}(Γ) is the substitution sending each generator of Δ to a cell of G(Γ) of the right boundary, then σ(c) ∈ G(Γ).

  Write G⁺(Γ) for the family defined in the same way with "forced" dropped from clause 3 (c the value of any witness x → y).


## 3. Questions

**Q1 (main: inhabitation by generated cells).** For every setting S ⊆ {left split, R}, every finite pasting diagram Γ and every dimension d: if x, y ∈ G(Γ) are parallel (d−1)-cells and F_{V+S}(Γ) has a witness x → y (a full-support cell; more generally, any cell x → y), does G(Γ) contain a cell x → y? A proof for all Γ, d and S, or a description of exactly which pairs (x, y) fail, is the main result wanted. A negative answer should give a pair (x, y) in G(Γ), with a cell x → y in F_{V+S}(Γ) and none in G(Γ).

**Q2 (main, the expected route).** Is every native letter of V + S either forced on its own arity, or such that its boundary pair on its arity is joined in F_{V+S} by a witness that is a composite of whiskered instances of letters of strictly smaller rank or of forced letters (so that the pair is inhabited in G by induction)? In particular: does every raising bridge b_a^ε of r5 Response 10 factor, up to a cell of F with the same endpoints, as an exchange followed by a smaller bridge, down to the seed, as B does in (N1)? If this holds, explain whether Q1 follows; if it fails, give the first failing letter.

**Q3 (fallback).** Is the bridge b : D⁰⁰X_v⁰ → X_u⁰D¹⁰ on ΣD⁴ ∨ ΣD³ of (N1) forced on its arity (by the test (N3))? If not, is its boundary pair inhabited in G? Equivalently for (N1): is the boundary pair of B inhabited in G?

## 4. What counts as a solution

A complete answer to Q1, positive or negative, in every dimension and setting; or a complete answer to Q2; or an answer to Q3.

# Researcher Response 1

**Q1 holds in every dimension for all four settings, including for cells of partial support.** In fact every path between generated vertices stays at generated vertices. Its individual steps can have values outside \(G\), but each step can be replaced by a generated cell with the same faces and Gray image. In Q3 the smaller bridge \(b\) is forced. Path (2) therefore inhabits the boundary of \(B\) in \(G\).

There are two qualifications to the proposed induction in Q2. The smaller-letter alternative holds in \(F\) with the rank specified below. The bypass need not be an exchange followed by another bridge. Also, above degree six a native letter can itself have a nongenerated face. We will use the alternative only after proving the assertion about intermediate vertices.

## A bypass on the two generating globes

Work with proper native calls, after evaluating the unit declarations. Use the actual arity of an interior bridge, restricting the fixed globe to the indicated hemisphere as in the declaration. At output degree \(n\), order calls lexicographically by
\[
                 (n-1-k,\ s+t),
\]
where \(k\) is the defining cut and \(s,t\) are the two input hom degrees there. Structural insertions are expanded as fixed actions. At a given degree this is a well-founded order on the native tags. We use the [constructor and equation tables](../answered/directed-interchange-all-degree-presentation-2026-11-22.md#1-constructors-and-boundaries) and the [signed raising recursion](../answered/lax-interchange-every-dimension-r5-2026-11-20.md#raising-a-globe-in-arbitrary-bidegree).

On the arity of a tag \(T\), no earlier cut has two proper composable spans. At the defining cut two proper operands must occupy the two short globes. On a single globe every proper cell is a named generator or face. Hence a step of at least the rank of \(T\) uses the same cut and the two top generators in the given order. There is no input generator of output degree \(n\).

A placement of **\(T\) itself** on a path between its generic endpoints must be the bare placement. Here are details of the padding test. Compare with the bare occurrence one pad rank \(i\) at a time, in increasing order. Once the lower pads are units, the two rank-\(i\) pads append their lengths to that of the bare \(i\)-row. Actions at higher ranks preserve this row length, since passing between the two \(i\)-faces of an \((i+1)\)-cell preserves it. For \(i\ge4\) this is the conserved positive grade \(m_i\). In ranks one and two use the number of traversed spans and the length in slots. At rank three on the two-globe diagram count generating advances and atomic crossings. A generating 4-globe changes one advance to another on the same slot, a square permutes events, and a prism or join carries the same advances and slot crossings on its two sides. Block subdivisions partition these crossings. For a prism the two layers of the moving input traverse the same slots, so its two block crossings have the same atomic length. These checks include the suspensions. Below \(n-1\) the path has the prescribed globular faces, and at \(n-1\) its vertices have the grade of the generic source. Thus at each rank the added length is zero. Positivity makes both pads units. The external prefix and suffix at the vertex rank are included in this test. [Row grades and structural actions](../answered/directed-interchange-all-degree-presentation-2026-11-22.md#3-a-box-for-the-operands-independent-of-the-top-word)

Every square and every unary cylinder is forced on its short arity, as are all seeds through relative hom output four. This uses the two unary schedules and the seed intervals for \(h,W,H_L,H_R,K,J\). The same placement checks hold after shifting the cut. The extra branch through the join factorization ends at the sink \(BAC\), and the short inputs admit no proper switched split in these tests. Their generic faces are generated recursively by the same lower heads and pure insertions. [Seed intervals and their suspensions](../answered/forced-lift-generation-2026-11-25.md#the-lower-faces-and-the-three-small-heads)

A variable on its globe has only its generating step. For the remaining tags we need to distinguish siblings of equal rank. On the two short globes read the disk tableau **at the defining cut**, with higher cuts strict. This is also a valid observation on a suspended disk. Indeed, cuts below its branching cut have only unit specializations. At the branching cut use the unshifted disk tables, and above it use their strict hom values. These respect the structural and unit formulas. The positive free-object equations above degree four are outer unary subdivisions and transports, with the other guards evaluated on their unit branches. The low equations at the branching cut are either the original Gray equations or, for a cut-one split, subdivision of the square on a composite edge, valid by bilinearity of its top chain and the common faces. Low equations involving an earlier cut specialize at units. Equations internal to the hom hold in the strict value. This gives the observation on the free tables for every \(S\). At cut zero it is precisely \(\pi\). At a positive cut the actual \(\pi\) already makes the disk operation an identity in the strict hom. [Free-object equations and the relative-cut tables](../answered/directed-interchange-all-degree-presentation-2026-11-22.md#2-relations-and-guards)

For a raising bridge or raised principal let \(v:x^0\to x^1\) be the moving generator. Contract \(v\) to the identity on \(x\), identifying its two faces. The bridge specializes to an identity. Consequently the image of *any* parallel path after contraction is an endomorphism in the loop-free tableau category, hence an identity. Every summand in the path's positive top chain must involve \(v\). Indeed every other basis pair maps to a basis pair under this contraction, with nonnegative coefficient. For a left raise the chain thus has the form \(v\otimes z\). Its differential in moving degree \(\deg(v)-1\) is \((x^1-x^0)\otimes z\), which determines \(z\) from the given endpoints before contraction. For a right raise the same component is \(z\otimes(x^1-x^0)\) with the common Koszul sign. Thus every such path has exactly the direct top chain. The lower rows are fixed by the endpoints. In particular the two interior siblings use distinct basis elements of the fixed globe, since \(a-p<q\) on their actual arity. Neither sibling can occur in a path for the other, even with pads, since fixed whiskering preserves the top chain and chronological composition adds positive chains.

For the remaining seeds \(E_{32},E_{23},E_{33},G,H\) the same calculation applies. Contract the varying top globe for a principal, the left top globe for \(G\), and the right one for \(H\). These are exactly the identity branches in the seed table. The only competing pair at the same output on the same ordered arity is \(G,H\), with respective top chains \(x\otimes B^1,A^1\otimes y\) in the notation of the double seed. This exhausts same-rank competitors. In the recursive families the new ordered bidegree fixes the direction of raising. Restriction at an interior row names the tag on the actual face, so it adds no second declaration.

The one-step path is full on the actual arity. The endpoints of the remaining tags differ by the nonzero differential of their direct basis pair in the relative tableau. If the tag is unforced, take a simple witness of different value. It cannot use the bare step, which would be the whole simple path. The preceding tests show that **every step has strictly smaller rank**, and that the bypass has the same \(\pi\)-value. This proves the letter alternative in Q2. It gives a finite way to find the bypass by (M6), without prescribing its length.

## Which tags can occur at intermediate vertices?

Call a degree-\(j\) native tag *allowed* if its bare value on its actual arity belongs to \(G\). This is a mathematical definition. The proof below needs no decision procedure for membership in \(G\). Globular variables are always allowed. Faces of generated cells are generated, by induction on clauses 1–4 using the structural face formulas and commutation of substitution with faces. Identities are available by substituting into the forced empty path on a globe.

For each potentially unallowed tag \(T\), the number of proper **top** occurrences of \(T\), ignoring its fields and pads, is monotone along directed paths one dimension up. The direction can depend on \(T\). To see this, (N1) disposes of absolute degrees through five. Above those degrees rigid factorization makes the count well defined. The outer unary equations only operate in the unary portions of a word. All unaries and the short heads through relative hom output four are allowed by the preceding seed checks. It remains to check the higher seeds, principals and interior bridges. [Rigid factorization](../answered/lax-interchange-every-dimension-r10-2026-11-20.md#researcher-response-2)

* In the \(E_{33}\) source the high seed occurrences are \(E_{23},G\), once each. In its target they are \(E_{32},H\), once each. The positional exchange contributes none of these. All other seed boundaries use lower heads. The change for each of these four tags therefore has a fixed sign.
* In a raised row the two copies of the **same** old hemisphere have identical tag counts. The contexts from below its immediate composition rank are fixed lower actions. At this rank the only additional top terms are the two preceding bridges. At the bottom they insert the same moving operand once each. Above the bottom they are proper interior tags. For a left raise the source contains the preceding \(b^1\) and the target the preceding \(b^0\). For a right raise the signs at adjacent ranks are opposite, so the source contains the preceding \(b^0\) and the target the preceding \(b^1\). Restriction of the fixed operand changes its layer or actual degree according to the declaration and preserves the tag's moving side and sign bit. Thus each interior tag can only be contributed on its designated side. A principal never serves as the preceding interior bridge. Its occurrences in an old hemisphere are carried on both sides.
* This calculation applies to **instances**. For proper operands, their generating faces remain proper at every positive rank. Substitution of such operands in the short disk preserves the proper or unit status of every generic subterm, by the effective-rank formulas. Hence the top calls in the two old layers keep their tags. A substituted input of the vertex rank can itself be a word containing \(T\). Such inputs occur equally often in the two faces. In the square, joins and variation seeds these are the pure updates, once on each side when present. In a raise the bottom insertion or the common old row carries them on both sides. All other fields have smaller degree. Shared exterior pads add the same counts. A unit instance is first evaluated to its declared word, and a globular generator has globular generators for faces. This covers every edge by path composition. [Effective ranks on free diagrams](../answered/lax-interchange-every-dimension-r10-2026-11-20.md#a-co-re-criterion-for-finite-equation-lists-preserving-effective-rank)

It follows that a tag absent from both endpoints, if unallowed, is absent at *every* intermediate vertex. This argument concerns the value of a vertex and works in the presence of directed loops.

## Induction on dimension

Write \(C_j\) for the \(j\)-cells whose two faces are generated and whose proper top letters all have allowed tags, counting a placed variable as allowed. We prove \(C_j=G_j\), that every path with generated \(j\)-vertices at both ends has generated vertices throughout, and the following reflection property. If a structural composite, fixed action or proper native call is generated, its constituent cells are generated, and in the native case its tag is allowed. These assertions hold through degree five by (N1). All statements concern every arity simultaneously.

Suppose they hold below \(j\). Consider a \(j\)-path with generated endpoints. By the vertex assertion at \(j-1\) every vertex is generated. In each placed step we can recover its pads as generated cells by applying lower-dimensional reflection to its two faces. At the immediate pad rank these faces use chronological products, and below it they use fixed actions. This also recovers the two faces of the unpadded step.

For a proper native step the same argument recovers **its operands and its generic faces** in the following sense. Spell its generic faces on the named globes, using their actual faces for projected variables and specializing units first. Each top input occurs as a whole variable in these expressions. Indeed, restricting to its short span gives the retained input under identities, whereas an expression using only its proper faces could not yield that input. This is the input-occurrence check for the actual arity, including the truncated fixed operand of a bridge. Descent through the instantiated expressions uses lower reflection for products, actions, identities and proper calls. Proper calls stay proper inside these expressions by the effective-rank calculation above. We therefore recover the images of both input variables as generated cells, and **each native tag used in the generic face expressions is allowed**. Rebuilding those expressions on the named variables, using the generated bare tags by substitution, gives generated generic faces. This last conclusion would not follow merely by inverting a substitution on equalities. [Operand occurrences in the rows](../answered/directed-interchange-all-degree-presentation-2026-11-22.md#3-a-box-for-the-operands-independent-of-the-top-word) · [Restriction to each short globe](../answered/forced-lift-generation-2026-11-25.md#which-cells-and-which-generic-letters)

In particular, if all the path's tags are allowed, each placed step is generated by substitution and whiskering. Hence \(C_j\subseteq G_j\). More generally, **every step on this path has a generated replacement with the same faces and Gray value**. Induct on the two-component rank in the bypass argument. For an allowed tag use the step itself. A forced tag in this position is allowed, since its generic faces have just been shown generated. For any remaining tag, substitute its operands in the smaller-rank bypass and attach the pads. All intermediate vertices of this replacement are again generated by the assertion at \(j-1\). The tuple substituted here is proper on both generating globes of the original tag. By preservation of effective ranks, every proper call of the normalized bypass stays proper and retains its cut, actual input degrees and tag. Its arity has no input variable of degree \(j\) that could expand to an unrestricted word. Thus the rank induction applies to the specialized path. Composition gives the claimed replacement. Ordinary input letters with their generated pads need no replacement. [Unit branches in the constructor table](../answered/directed-interchange-all-degree-presentation-2026-11-22.md#1-constructors-and-boundaries)

It remains to prove the other inclusion \(G_j\subseteq C_j\). The face condition holds by face closure. Use induction on a generation derivation for the top-tag condition.

1. Variables, composites and whiskerings preserve it. Chronological composition concatenates lists, and lower fixed actions transport top occurrences.
2. At a forced introduction take a full witness and replace each of its steps as above, retaining a spelling made of **allowed placed letters** by the rank induction. The replacement is still full. In a positive Gray tableau the downward-closed support of a composite is the union of the supports of its pieces. The higher chains add, and an intermediate-row term either survives outside or lies in the boundary support of a higher term. Thus replacing by the same Gray value preserves witness support. Erasing cycles preserves fullness by §2 and leaves a word of allowed letters. Forcedness identifies its value with the value being introduced. Rigid factorization preserves the asserted absence of unallowed tags.
3. At a substitution evaluate a collected spelling. A placed variable is replaced by its supplied generated image, to which the derivation induction applies at degree \(j\). A surviving proper native call keeps its allowed tag. It matters here that unit specializations cannot introduce an **unallowed** top tag. To check this, perform the same unit specialization first on named globes of the surviving effective degrees. The tuple there consists of variables and iterated identities on them, hence is generated. For a raised auxiliary the proper result, if present at output degree, is precisely the bare bridge on the smaller fixed globe, including its principal or unary at the end of the range. Its bare value is therefore generated by clause 4 applied to the originally allowed bare tag. A unit moving variation leaves an identity. The seed branches either give one of the surviving bare heads or use the explicitly generated low seeds and their actions, including the paste for \(J\). Further unit layers iterate these same cases. Substitution of the remaining proper operands now preserves the surviving tags. Structural pads and unary subdivisions introduce no unallowed tag. This checks substitution even when the original ambient diagram has unused higher generators.

With \(G_j=C_j\) proved, take any path between generated \(j\)-vertices. Every intermediate vertex has their same lower faces, and its unallowed counts are zero by monotonicity. It belongs to \(C_j=G_j\). Finally, reflection at \(j\) follows for a chronological product by this vertex assertion **one rank lower** and inheritance of top tags by each factor. For a fixed action, reflect its lower face actions (products at the immediate rank) to recover the pad and the underlying faces, and read the unchanged top tags of the acted-on word. A product of equal-dimensional cells at a lower cut expands into a chronological product of fixed actions, so these two reflection arguments recover both factors. For an identity read its base. For a proper call read its top tag and recover its operands from its faces as above. This finishes the simultaneous induction. The replacement assertion at degree \(d\), composed along any given path, proves Q1. If the original path was full the replacement is full as well.

## The smaller bridge and the limit of the exchange formula

On \(\Sigma D^4\vee\Sigma D^3\), mark a top occurrence in a degree-five vertex precisely when it is the cut-zero \(J(x^0,y)\), ignoring its pads. This count is well defined by rigid factorization. It is one at \(D^{00}X_v^0\) and zero at \(X_u^0D^{10}\). In the (N3) graph the only edges that can **decrease** the count are placements of \(b\). To verify the claim, inspect the degree-six heads as follows.

* At a positive cut, the two faces use only positive-cut heads and pure operand insertions. Any cut-zero \(J\) comes from a rank-five input or a pad. Such an input is carried unchanged on both sides, including the fields \(x^0,y\) internal to its occurrences.
* At cut zero the short operands are named faces. Unaries and \(E_{32},E_{23}\) have no \(J\) in their source. The seed moves \(G:L\to D\), \(H:R\to D\) introduce \(J\) in their target. At raising row \(a=4\) the moving degree must be four, possible only on the left here. For fixed degree two neither hemisphere of the \((3,2)\) seed uses \(J\). For fixed degree three the old negative row \(P\) has none and the positive row \(D=c_x b_x J f_y\) has exactly one. Passing from layer zero to layer one in that row is exactly \(b\). These are all proper calls of the required output, including after evaluation of units.

By the padding test, a placement of \(b\) on this interval is the direct edge. Omitting its value leaves the marked count nondecreasing from one, so the target of count zero is unreachable. This is a cut certificate for (N3) in all four graphs. The direct step has full support, and its degree-five faces are generated. Thus \(b\) is forced and generated. The positional exchange in (2) is a placed instance of the forced square, so (2) is the promised generated witness for \(B\). No route at all bypasses \(b\) by an exchange and a smaller bridge. It is already at the first possible output of a proper interior raising bridge, since \(p\ge3\) and \(a>p\).

If “the pair is inhabited in \(G\)” in Q2 is required even for native letters with arbitrary faces, its first failure is one degree higher. On the \((4,4)\) pair take the left-raise \(b_5^0\) with old moving degree three. Its source is \(C_4^0[r_5^0(x^0)]\) followed at hom cut four by \(b_4^1=B\). This degree-six source has positive \(B\)-count, so it lies outside \(G\) by the all-arity count in (N1). Face closure precludes a generated cell with that source. Through output six all native faces are generated by (N1), so Q1 supplies a generated inhabitant there. This explains why smaller-rank bypasses alone do not prove Q1 and why its hypothesis on the two endpoints matters.
