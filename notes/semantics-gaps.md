# Long-term semantics gaps

*Ledger item E11 (2026-11-18). This note records what is not yet known about the models of CaTT-style theories over ωCat (P, L, and CaTT itself), so that no note has to assume it silently. These are long-term problems. Each section says what is known (with sources), what is open, and what can be done now. Markers: **[paper]** checked against the source, with the place; **[proved]** argued here; **[conj]** open.*

Orientation conventions are in `conventions.md`.

---

## 1. What a "model" means at each level

| Level | P (CaTT ambient) | L (two-tier Gray-style core) |
|---|---|---|
| Algebraic models | Grothendieck–Maltsiniotis weak ω-categories: presheaves on the CaTT coherator that send globular sums to globular products. Models of CaTT are exactly these **[paper]** (Benjamin–Finster–Mimram, [2106.04475](https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.04475), abstract and §3). | Not defined yet. The expected notion is a "Gray coherator", a category of L-contexts and terms, with models the presheaves that send wedges of suspensions to the corresponding limits. |
| Strict models | Strict ω-categories. In strict ω-Cat with its **cartesian** closed structure, 2-cells between functors are *strict* natural transformations. | Strict Gray-enriched categories. **[proved, given `pseudo-vs-gray.md` §3.6 Theorem 1]** AM's ∞-Cat_lax (strict ω-categories, strict functors, lax transformations; AM Ex. C.18) is one, with the orientation of `conventions.md` §4. So **strict ω-Cat with lax transformations is a model of two-tier L**. |
| Homotopical models | (∞,ω)-categories, through some comparison with GM weak ω-categories (§2). | Oriented (∞,ω)-categories in Gepner–Heine's sense ([2510.10504](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.10504)), conditionally on §2 (`pseudo-vs-gray.md` §3.6 Theorem 2). |

The table already shows the first gap: **P's intended model (ω-categories with pseudo transformations) is not a strict model.** Strict ω-Cat with cartesian homs has strict transformations, and pseudo transformations only form a weak structure. So P is tested strictly only on the wrong 2-cells, while L has a strict model with exactly its intended 2-cells.

## 2. Interpreting a dependent type theory in (∞,ω)-categories

**The gap.** CaTT and L are dependent type theories with strict substitution. Interpreting them in an (∞,ω)-category requires choosing composites and coherence cells coherently, so that substitution is strictly functorial. This is the "strictification / coherent choice" problem.
- For CaTT it amounts to comparing GM weak ω-categories (algebraic, strict substitution) with (∞,ω)-categories (homotopical). No such comparison is known to us.
- It is shared by P and L. `pseudo-vs-gray.md` §3.6 (caveat after Theorem 2) and §5.4 record it.

**What is known.**
- *Strict ω-categories.* Lafont–Métayer–Worytkiewicz give a model structure on strict ω-Cat, cofibrantly generated by the boundary inclusions ∂Oₙ → Oₙ **[paper]** ([0712.0617](https://arxiv.org/abs/0712.0617), §4, "I = {iₙ}").
- *Weak ω-categories (Batanin–Leinster).* Leinster's operad is the universal cofibrant replacement of the strict ω-category operad for the weak factorisation system generated by boundary inclusions **[paper]** (Garner, [0804.2663](https://arxiv.org/abs/0804.2663), abstract). Markakis extends this to generalised signatures: free algebras on computads are the cofibrant objects **[paper]** ([2303.11978](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.11978), abstract).
- *No model structure on weak ω-categories yet.* Fujii–Hoshino–Maehara prove 2-out-of-3 for ω-weak equivalences between Batanin–Leinster weak ω-categories, and call it "a necessary step towards the construction of a (left semi-)model structure", which is not yet constructed **[paper]** ([2406.13240](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.13240) v3, introduction).
- *(∞,ω)-categories.* Cat_ω is presentable and cartesian closed (Loubaton, [2406.05425](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.05425), Prop. 2.2.1.54 and the definition after it) **[paper]**, and has Campion's Gray tensor (`conventions.md` §2).

**Answered in part (2026-11-18).** The researcher response, verified, is `conjectures/answered/catt-models-in-infinity-omega-2026-11-18.md`. A round-2 prompt for what remains is `conjectures/outbox/catt-models-in-infinity-omega.md`.
- **(S1), homotopy theory: localisation exists, identification open.** Mod(CaTT) ≃ Batanin–Leinster weak ω-categories, through computads **[paper]** (Benjamin–Markakis–Sarti, [2405.00398](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.00398), §7). So the FHM ω-weak equivalences W transfer to CaTT models. They satisfy 2-out-of-3 and 2-out-of-6, and agree with the folk (LMW) equivalences on strict ω-categories **[paper]** ([2406.13240](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.13240), Thm 3.3.3, Cor 3.3.4, PDF numbering). Hence the relative-category localisation 𝓗_CaTT = Mod(CaTT)[W⁻¹] exists. There is still no model or semi-model structure, and no theorem identifying 𝓗_CaTT with any geometric Cat_ω **[conj]**. FHM's ω-equifibrations ([2511.09849](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.09849), Prop. 4.1.5, Thm 4.3.1) are the candidate fibrations.
- **(S2), coherent choice: one direction done.** From an algebraically fibrant complete Segal Θ-space Y (Nikolaus's algebraic fibrant replacement) one builds a set-valued CaTT model S(Y), with strict substitution, S(Y)ₙ = (UY)(Dₙ)₀. S is homotopical, so it induces Cat_ω^ind → 𝓗_CaTT. The key step is that the universal pasting resolutions map to the representables yP by trivial fibrations, because yP is discrete and fibrant. This is a deduction in the response from cited machinery **[proved there, checked]**. Reconstruction, i.e. that the counit is a weak equivalence, is **open [conj]**. Unconditionally, the 2-truncated case, which includes (2,2)-categories, is presented by bicategories **[paper]** (Campbell, [1907.01999](https://arxiv.org/abs/1907.01999), Thm 7.28, Thms 8.7, 8.12).
- **(S3), which notion: the raw inductive nerve is incompatible with W.** The coherent walking ω-equivalence E is a strict polygraph whose map E → 1 is a folk weak equivalence **[paper]** (Hadzihasanovic–Loubaton–Ozornova–Rovelli, [2404.14509](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.14509), Rem. 1.29, Thm 1.33). E is also gaunt (generator count). So no functor 𝓗_CaTT → Cat_ω^ind restricts to the raw inductive (Rezk-completed strict Θ-) nerve on strict ω-categories **[proved there, checked]**. The intended target is therefore the coinductive notion, or the inductive one with coinductively invertible cells marked: the marked Street nerve preserves folk equivalences **[paper]** (Henry–Loubaton, [2301.11424](https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.11424), Prop. 4.58). This settles the "coinductive vs inductive" question of §3 for CaTT in that direction.
- **(S4), free shapes: they agree.** Finite globular pasting shapes and their Gray products give the same free objects in the inductive and coinductive theories. This uses Campion, [2311.00205](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.00205), Cor. 3.5, Thm 3.14, Cor. 3.15, under his site hypotheses **[paper]**. Constructions with a uniform dimension bound can be done in Cat_n and included, by Loubaton's adjoint triple τᵢₙ ⊣ iₙ ⊣ τₙ. Unbounded constructions and mapping spaces into arbitrary targets can differ **[paper, per the response]**.

**What is still open.**
- (S1′) A model structure on CaTT models with weak equivalences W, or an identification of 𝓗_CaTT with a geometric theory (coinductive, or inductive with markings).
- (S2′) Reconstruction: whether S is essentially surjective onto 𝓗_CaTT, so that every (∞,ω)-category is presented by a strict-substitution CaTT model. The oriented version for L (Gepner–Heine) is untouched.
- (S3′) A precise statement of which geometric theory CaTT presents. The marked-inductive and coinductive candidates remain.

**What can be done now.** Every claim in the notes that needs an (∞,ω) model is stated *conditionally* on (S2), and is checked in the strict models of §1 first. The Agda formalisation targets the syntax and the strict models only. For L the strict model is now formalized: `src/CaTTExp/Strict/` proves that every derivable L-term over a pasting context is a cell of the free strict Gray-enriched category ν(K(Γ)), and that no term has the reverse interchanger's type. Two facts about Steiner complexes are explicit hypotheses there: support determines cells, and the total cell of a Gray tensor is a cell (`agda-gray-findings.md`, last section).

## 3. Which (∞,ω)-categories?

**The gap.** There is more than one notion of (∞,ω)-category.
- Loubaton uses the **inductive** one, Cat_ω = lim Cat_n **[paper]** ([2406.05425](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.05425), introduction, citing Schommer-Pries and Rezk for the plurality).
- Campion's Gray tensor is built on **flagged** (∞,∞)-categories Cat^f_ω, then transferred to localizations including Cat_n for n ≤ ω and the version with **coinductive** equivalences **[paper]** ([2311.00205](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.00205), remarks after Theorem A, and Outline).
- CaTT's invertibility is **coinductive** (Benjamin–Markakis, [2406.12127](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.12127)).

**What is open.**
- (S3) Which notion is the intended semantics of CaTT over ωCat, and of L. Partly answered (§2): CaTT's weak equivalences rule out the raw inductive nerve, so the target is coinductive, or inductive with coinductive markings. The limit and Yoneda results we cite (Loubaton) are for the inductive one, so using them needs the marked version or a transfer.
- (S4) Whether the results the notes rely on hold in the chosen notion. For finite free shapes and their Gray products, yes in both (§2). `free-infinity-gray-of-pasting-diagrams.md` is solved in Cat^f_ω and transferred to Cat_n (its "Literature status"). The oriented-limits question (`conjectures/oriented-lax-limits.md`, B8) should state which notion it uses.

## 4. Is ωCat itself a model?

**The gap.** The project's long-term goal is an internal language for the ω-category of ω-categories. That needs ωCat itself, with ⋆ interpreted as ω-categories, to be a model.

**Status by option.**
- **L, strict.** Yes (§1): ∞-Cat_lax models two-tier L, given Theorem 1 of `pseudo-vs-gray.md` §3.6, whose combinatorial lemmas are discharged for the needed case. This is a large model, which is harmless because the theory has no universes.
- **P, strict.** Strict ω-Cat models CaTT, but with strict 2-cells (§1). The intended ambient, with pseudo transformations, is not strict.
- **Both, (∞,ω).** Open, by §2 and §3.
- **Uniform L.** Open: the strict structure is not constructed yet (E6).
- **With limits.** Open: whether oriented ∞-categories satisfy the unrestricted lax universal property is B8 (`conjectures/oriented-lax-limits.md`, out to a researcher).

## 5. Other semantic gaps recorded elsewhere

- **Attaching cells in the weak model** (`free-objects.md` §3.6). The strict model is the pushout along ∂Dⁿ → Dⁿ (LMW's generating cofibrations). In the (∞,ω) model it is the pushout in Cat_ω, which exists by presentability. For GM weak ω-categories there is no model structure to make "homotopy pushout" precise (§2).
- **Universes.** Excluded by design (project standard 5). Loubaton's Yoneda lemma uses ω as a universe (`limits-representables.md` §2.2), so the notes use only representability statements.
- **Gray-coherator models of L** (`pseudo-vs-gray.md` §5.4). Markakis's generalised signatures ([2303.11978](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.11978)) cover signatures whose sorts form a direct category, including weak ω-categories. Whether L's theory is such a signature, so that its models have a computad description, is open **[conj]**. It needs L's coh rule to be stated as operations over computads, which the Agda work (LCORE) may supply.
