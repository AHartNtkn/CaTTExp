# Soundness of CaTT in arbitrary strict ω-categories (ledger A3a, general case)

Owner: META. Started 2026-11-18. This note covers the literature, the definitions, the proof route and the formalisation plan. Progress is tracked in `open-issues.md` (row A3a).

## 1. What is already done, and what is left

`CaTTExp.Strict.Sound` (PR #106) proves soundness of CaTT, constants included, in ν(K) for **every** chain complex with a basis K. The ν(K) are Steiner's strict ω-categories. They include the free strict ω-categories on loop-free complexes (orientals, cubes, Gray tensors of pasting diagrams). They do **not** include every strict ω-category: a strict ω-category with a non-trivial loop, or one with equations between composites, is not of the form ν(K).

What is left is soundness in an arbitrary strict ω-category C, given by the usual algebraic axioms. The statement is: for every derivable `Γ ⊢ t : A` and every environment ρ of cells of C whose boundaries match Γ, the interpretation ⟦t⟧ρ exists. It is a cell of C whose boundaries are ⟦s⟧ρ and ⟦u⟧ρ when A = s →_B u.

## 2. Literature

- **Definition of strict ω-category.** We use the single-set definition: a set C, idempotent boundary maps s_n, t_n : C → C, and partial compositions #_n defined when t_n x = s_n y. The axioms are boundary, unit, associativity and interchange. Identities are implicit: x is an identity in dimension n+1 when s_n x = x = t_n x. See Street, "The algebra of oriented simplexes" (JPAA 49, 1987), §1, and Steiner, "Omega-categories and chain complexes" (HHA 6, 2004; arXiv math/0403237), Def. 2.1. This definition avoids identity padding when composing cells of different dimensions, which simplifies pasting composites considerably. It is equivalent to the globular definition (Leinster, *Higher Operads, Higher Categories*, arXiv math/0305049, Ch. 1.4 and App. F; Steiner loc. cit.).
- **Why strict ω-categories are CaTT models.** Models of CaTT are the Grothendieck–Maltsiniotis weak ω-categories (Benjamin–Finster–Mimram, arXiv 2106.04475). A strict ω-category is such a model because the free strict ω-category on a globular pasting diagram is contractible in the strong sense: every admissible pair has a unique lift. Ara proves that Joyal's Θ is a pseudo-coherator of ∞-categories (Ara, thesis, 2010, Ch. 5, §5.1: "tout couple admissible de Θ admet un unique relèvement"). The groupoid analogue, strict ∞-groupoids are Grothendieck ∞-groupoids, is Ara, arXiv 1212.3085. It uses weak contractibility of free strict ∞-groupoids on globular pasting schemes, proved with Métayer/Lafont–Métayer–Worytkiewicz cylinders.
- **The pasting theorem.** Every labelled globular pasting diagram in a strict ω-category has a unique composite, and composites of composites are composites. This is the free strict ω-category monad on globular sets: labelled Batanin trees, with μ given by grafting (Batanin 1998; Leinster, App. F and §8.1). For n-categories it is Power's pasting theorem ("An n-categorical pasting theorem", 1991). A chain-complex proof is Steiner 2004, Thms 5.6, 5.7 and 6.1: for K with a loop-free unital basis, ν(K) is composition-generated by its atoms and presented by them. For K = λΘ, Θ a pasting context, this says ν(λΘ) is the free strict ω-category on the globular set Θ.
- **Formalisations.** The Isabelle AFP entry *Strict Omega Categories* (Bordg and Doña Mateo) defines strict ω-categories and the strict ω-category of pasting diagrams, as a step towards Batanin–Leinster weak ω-categories. We found no formalisation of the pasting theorem or of "strict ω-categories are CaTT models".

## 3. Two design points

**Partial composition.** C's composition is partial. A raw-term interpretation (as in `Strict.Interp`, where ν(K)'s operations are total) would need either decidable equality of cells or a total extension of #_n, and either would be an extra hypothesis. Instead, the interpretation is a **relation** `Ev ρ t c` ("t evaluates to c in ρ"), with composites taking their composability proofs as arguments. Soundness then states existence, and a separate lemma states determinism. With K available (the library is `--safe` with K), composites do not depend on the composability proofs.

**No universes in the object language.** The definition of C is an ordinary Agda record over `Set` in the metatheory, like `Signature`. CaTT itself is unchanged.

## 4. Proof route

1. **Labelled trees.** A C-labelled Batanin tree at level k is `node x₀ [(T₁, x₁), …, (T_m, x_m)]`. Here the x_i are cells (the vertices at level k), and each T_i is a labelled tree at level k+1 whose root label is the (k+1)-cell between x_{i−1} and x_i. Its composite is a fold:
   - comp(node x₀ []) = x₀
   - comp(node x₀ ((T, x) ∷ r)) = comp T #_k comp(node x r)

   Validity says that the labels have the boundaries their positions prescribe.
2. **Boundary lemma.** s_j comp(T) = comp(∂⁻_j T) and t_j comp(T) = comp(∂⁺_j T), where ∂^±_j truncates the tree at height j and keeps the first (respectively last) vertex at height j. Validity of T gives composability at every #.
3. **Binary pasting lemma.** If ∂⁺_k S₁ = ∂⁻_k S₂, then comp(S₁ ⊔_k S₂) = comp S₁ #_k comp S₂. Gluing is defined by recursion: ⊔_0 concatenates the root lists, and ⊔_{k+1} zips the subtrees with ⊔_k. The proof is by induction on k and on the trees:
   - k = 0 is associativity of #_0;
   - k > 0 is the inductive hypothesis inside each branch, then interchange of #_0 with #_k, iterated along the root list.
4. **Functor out of the free object.** For a pasting context Θ and a valid labelling λ of Θ in C, define F_λ on cells of ν(λΘ) as the composite of the sub-pasting diagram of Θ spanned by the cell. Cells of ν(λΘ) are determined by their supports (closed, `conjectures/steiner-support-determines-cells.md`, ledger G3), and supports of cells of ν(λΘ) are sub-pasting diagrams. By the binary pasting lemma, F_λ preserves #_k. It sends the atom of x to λ(x).
5. **Interpretation commutes with F.** For a term t over a pasting context Θ, Ev λ t c implies c = F_λ(⟦t⟧^ν η_Θ), where the right side uses A3a's ν-interpretation at the generic environment. The proof is by induction on t:
   - the var case is the atom equation;
   - the coh case uses that the ν-interpretation of a coh is the ν-pasting composite of its substitution, and that F_λ commutes with pasting composites (step 3, folded over the tree).
6. **Soundness.** For a coh over Θ with substitution σ, the coh is sent to F_{⟦σ⟧ρ}(c_Θ), where c_Θ is the coh at η_Θ. That is a cell of ν(λΘ) by A3a. Its boundaries are F of the boundaries, which are ⟦s⟧ and ⟦u⟧ by step 5. Constants are handled as in `Strict.Sound`, by the hypothesis that each constant is a cell with its type's boundary.

Steps 1–3 are independent of CaTT and are the expected bulk of the formalisation. Steps 4–5 reuse A3a's support lemma and need the dictionary between cells of ν(λΘ) and sub-pasting diagrams of Θ. An outside prompt asking for a shorter route through steps 3–5 was answered and verified: `conjectures/answered/strict-omega-pasting-formal-2026-11-18.md` (step 4 can be dropped; see its verification section).
