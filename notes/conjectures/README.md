# Conjectures and open problems

**The repository copy (`notes/conjectures/` on `main` of AHartNtkn/CaTTExp) is canonical.** The shared project folder holds a synced copy. Edits made there are folded back into the repository.

This folder holds problem statements written to be **self-contained**: a reader who knows higher category theory but nothing about this project should be able to work on any file here alone.

**Project standard (adopted 2026-11-18).** "Unproved" is a reason to investigate, never a reason to reject a design. When a design note rests on an unproved claim:
1. The note attempts a proof and records how far the attempt got.
2. Where the attempt stalls, the remaining gap is written up here as a well-posed problem.

Each file has the same parts:
- **Background:** the definitions needed, with references.
- **Statement:** precise, with any choices of convention made explicit.
- **What is known:** related results and partial arguments.
- **Why it matters:** one paragraph, with no project jargon.
- **What would count as a solution:** a proof, a counterexample, or a reference.

Literature searches were done on 2026-11-18. Each file ends with a "Literature status" or "Literature search" section, with citations.

In ∞Cat as an oriented category the unrestricted lax universal property is consistent, lax limits exist, and the corrected partially lax limit (coherently marked leg) is the full hom-object. Problem 3 as literally stated is false.
  - `steiner-support-determines-cells.md`: the main case follows from Hadzihasanovic's book (arXiv 2404.07273), Prop. 8.3.17 and Thm 11.2.18.
  - `single-hom-conservativity.md`: proved in Agda (2026-11-18), both halves, for the empty signature (`Gray/SuppVars.agda`, `Strict/SuppFV.agda`).
- **Partly solved:**
  - `finite-fibration-test.md`: solved for set-valued weights over graphs, and (researcher, verified 2026-11-18) for Cat-valued weights with finitely generated free values over a graph; (Loc) proved. Open: 2-dimensional bases and beyond. (Higher-base rounds through r6, verified 2026-11-18: FT₃ for positive 2-computads, FT_{3,m} for 3-dimensional bases under item 23's hypotheses, FT_{n,m} for suspension bases; general n ≥ 4 open.)
  - `planar-pasting-types.md`: PL1 is proved directly (`closed-structure.md` Part A) and units have a contractible criterion (Part B). PL-ω is open, with a candidate criterion that passes the first test (§4.1).
  - `lax-colimit-of-point.md`: the semantic claim is solved (Loubaton, Example 4.2.3.12). The syntactic form is false for arbitrary cone-judgment derivations (verified counterexample); it is open for a specified cocone construction.
  - `gray-contexts-in-catt.md`: G(2) is proved (researcher, verified; also in-house), with existence in dimension 3, so Gray generators of dimension ≤ 4 are typed. Mikhail's printed Gray rules are defective (verified). §6 (3), the (∞,ω) part, is answered. Open: G(n ≥ 3) uniqueness, G-alg, and §6 (1)–(2). Second researcher response (verified 2026-11-18, `answered/gray-contexts-2026-11-18.md`) settles the identity-boundary case of RC(3) for the lax square and reduces the cube to its negative hemisphere. Through round 8 (verified 2026-11-18): RC(3) for the Gray cube and the 2 × 2 and 2 × 3 grids, and a conditional 4-dimensional extension theorem; open: general dimension-2 exchange (3 × 3 grid), RC(4), G(n ≥ 3).
  - `naturality-arbitrary-depth.md`: every 2-dimensional coherence over a fully selected chain (the associator included) is settled by a researcher's relative ladder theorem, verified here. Every term of dimension ≤ 2, with any up-closed set, is now settled (`../relative-coherence.md`). **Round 8 (verified 2026-11-18): RC(3)(Q↑Y) for every pasting context Q and up-closed Y, so every coherence of dimension ≤ 3 is settled.** Open: dimension ≥ 4 (RC(4), smallest case the 3-disc cylinder) and global K. **2026-11-19:** maximal-variable naturality holds at every term depth (`answered/relative-coherence-all-dimensions-2026-11-19.md`), which settles context depth 0 at any term depth (§4 case 1); the general case is RC_id(∞), `outbox/relative-coherence-all-dimensions-r2.md`.
  - `strict-lax-interchange-at-every-level.md`: the literal reading (raw higher interchange cells) is proved impossible by a researcher, verified here; the statement is corrected to boundary-adjusted higher comparisons, which remain open.
- **Open:**
  - `vertical-composition-by-lifts.md` (VL): is the naturality-lift construction of vertical composition of lax transformations well typed in every dimension? Dimension 3 accepted by the certified checker in a compiled run; dimension 4 checked on raw types (2026-11-19).
  - `cone-over-coherences.md` (N^cone): do cone cells exist over every coherence term, so that the specified cone extends to every context? Dimensions 1 and 2 proved; dimension n reduces to RC(1..n) on join contexts (researcher, verified 2026-11-18).
  - `lax-functor-refinement.md`: answered (researcher, verified 2026-11-18). LF is false as stated (fillers are not unique; homs of orientals are not Gray cubes), but every filler is determined by its support S and there is an exact finite test; the rule must be `dcoh_S`.
  - `cartesian-closed-higher-coherence.md`: answered (researcher, verified 2026-11-18): Q1–Q2 proved, Q3 proved over graphs; open: Dir with non-invertible higher hypotheses.
  - `non-derivability.md`: three questions for a CaTT-style theory with limits, colimits and free objects: is recursion on ℕ = Hom(x, x) derivable, is ℕ provably a set, and are quotient sets provably effective? Answered (researcher, verified 2026-11-18): no to Q1 and Q3, even with exponentials by shapes; no to Q2 only for enriched cotensors. Q2 under exponentials: also no (`answered/non-derivability-q2-2026-11-18.md`).
